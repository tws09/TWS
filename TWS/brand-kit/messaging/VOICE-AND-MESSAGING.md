# HousesBase Voice and Messaging

## Foundations

- Positioning: The operating base for modern software houses.
- Signature: One system for projects, people, and margin.
- Hero: Run projects, people, clients, and margin from one connected base.
- Descriptor: Software House OS
- Primary CTA: Get started

Do not publish free-trial or “no credit card required” claims until the trial is
implemented and operationally confirmed.

## Voice

- Confident
- Precise
- Trustworthy
- Proof-led
- Operational

Use concrete nouns and active verbs. Lead with visible operational outcomes.
Avoid unsupported claims, surveillance language, buzzwords, and inflated
enterprise phrasing.

## Product messages

- Delivery: Projects move. Everyone sees why.
- People: Capacity without the guesswork.
- Finance: Know the margin before month-end.
- Platform: Built to run the full delivery engine.
- Nucleus: Project-aware assistance inside HousesBase.

## Brand pillars

1. One operational record
2. Built specifically for software houses
3. Delivery connected to people and margin
4. Control without employee surveillance
5. Dedicated company workspaces

## Approved calls to action

- Get started
- Explore the platform
- Book a walkthrough
- Talk to sales
