# HousesBase Brand Guidelines

## Identity concept

The HousesBase gateway combines an H-like structure, doorway, and operational
base. Its orange center pane represents the connection through which separate
parts of a software house become one working system.

The signature wordmark carries that connection into the beginning of the B.
The small terminal dot adds a controlled digital signature. HousesBase must
always read as one name.

## Lockup hierarchy

### Full marketing lockup

Refined gateway + signature wordmark + `Software House OS`.

- Minimum width: 360 px
- Use on campaign, website-hero, sales, event, and formal brand material
- Use `full-light` on white, mist, and pale neutral surfaces
- Use `full-dark` on navy and dark surfaces

### Signature lockup

Refined gateway + hinge-and-dot wordmark, without descriptor.

- Minimum width: 240 px
- Use in marketing headers and brand-led applications with sufficient space
- Never reconstruct the hinge or dot in CSS

### Compact lockup

Refined gateway + uninterrupted HousesBase wordmark, without descriptor,
hinge, or dot.

- Minimum width: 160 px
- Use in compact navigation and constrained digital headers
- Below 160 px, use the standalone mark

### Standalone mark

- Minimum size: 24 px high
- Use the dedicated favicon at 16–32 px
- Use the mark for collapsed navigation, favicon, and app-icon contexts

## Accessibility

Treat every complete lockup as one image. Use the accessible name:

`HousesBase — Software House OS`

For descriptor-free lockups use `HousesBase`. Internal gateway, wordmark,
hinge, dot, and descriptor paths are decorative and must not be announced
separately.

All product and marketing use targets WCAG 2.2 AA.

- Primary buttons: white text on navy
- Orange `#F04E25`: artwork, hinge, dot, icons, and decorative detail
- Small orange text on light backgrounds: `#B83515`
- Do not use white normal text on orange, support blue, or success green
- Never communicate status with color alone
- Focus indicators must remain visible at a minimum 3:1 boundary contrast

## Backgrounds

- Light: navy artwork with orange pane, hinge, and dot
- Dark: white artwork with orange pane, hinge, and dot
- Mist: use the light artwork
- Photography: use only on a calm image region with sufficient contrast or a
  navy overlay; do not place artwork over high-frequency detail
- Print: use the monochrome or grayscale test asset when spot color is not
  available

## Clear space

Keep clear space around a lockup equal to at least one quarter of the gateway’s
rendered height. Do not allow text, borders, photography subjects, or interface
controls into this area.

## Tenant identity

Inside a tenant workspace, the tenant logo and name lead. Use a discreet
`Powered by HousesBase` endorsement on tenant login, onboarding, client-facing
surfaces, and other appropriate attribution points.

## Nucleus

Nucleus is a named HousesBase capability, not a separate master brand. It uses
HousesBase typography and foundations, with support blue as its conversational
accent. Do not create an independent Nucleus master logo.

## Prohibited use

- Do not write the public brand as TWS or The Wolf Stack.
- Do not introduce Delta Labs attribution at this stage.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, crop, or redraw the mark.
- Do not type a replacement wordmark.
- Do not separate “Houses” and “Base” into two brands.
- Do not use the hinge and dot below 240 px.
- Do not add gradients, glows, outlines, or shadows to the logo artwork.
- Do not claim trademark clearance; legal review remains separate.
