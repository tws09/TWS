# Direction C refinement source

The review wordmark is built from outlined Manrope ExtraBold letterforms,
manually kerned as a single HousesBase name, then combined with custom vector
hinge and terminal-dot details.

The outlined `Software House OS` descriptor is derived from JetBrains Mono
SemiBold and is packaged as vector geometry in the full production lockups.

The Manrope and JetBrains Mono sources are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. The production SVGs contain letter outlines only; they do not
embed or redistribute font software.

These assets are refinement drafts, not final production exports. The selected
wordmark will receive a final optical pass and trademark review before release.
